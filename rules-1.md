# Rules, Policies, and Conduct

★ DISCLAIMER ★

Ignorance of these rules will not be tolerated.

By submitting a whitelist application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by these rules.

★ ATTENTION! ★

These rules are subject to change at any time. Anything not covered here is handled on a case-by-case basis. If you are unsure about something, please make a ticket.

Staff has final say for any decision made.

Thank you.

★ NOTICE ★

The Lonesome Trail is an 18+ community.
You and your character must both be at least 18 years old.
Character names must not reference famous or well-known people from history, movies, games, books, or other media.
Character names and in-character speech must fit the Wild West era, circa 1900.
This server follows its own rules and guidelines, not personal interpretations of fairness.

★ ZERO-TOLERANCE RULES ★

Violations of these rules may result in an automatic permanent ban unless staff decides otherwise.

* Exploiting: Any use of game exploits or unintended mechanics.
* Discriminatory language or actions: Slurs or derogatory remarks targeting race, ethnicity, orientation, or disability.
* Sexual harassment: Unwanted or inappropriate comments, actions, or advances of a sexual nature.
* Stalking or predatory behavior: Creepy, threatening, or unwarranted attention toward members.
* Suicide roleplay: Any depiction or discussion is prohibited.
* Third-party communication: IC communication must stay in game.
* Roleplay bleed: OOC emotions may not negatively affect IC roleplay.

★ GENERAL RULES ★

* Account security: Do not share Steam accounts.
* Respect and conduct: Treat all members with respect. Harassment, trolling, sexism, racism, and hate speech are not allowed.
* Do not discuss politically charged or inflammatory topics.
* No spamming or self-promotion without explicit approval from staff.
* Content restrictions: NSFW or obscene content is strictly prohibited.
* Reporting issues: If you encounter rule-breaking or feel unsafe, submit a ticket.
* Discord nicknames: Your Discord nickname must reflect your primary character name.
* Rule acknowledgment: You must read and understand all rules before entering the server.
* Communication with staff: Do not DM staff unless they request it. Use the ticket system for support.
* Gameplay restrictions: No macros and no third-party crosshairs.
* AFK behavior: Log out if you need to step away. Being AFK does not exempt you from interactions.

★ SERVER RULES ★

* Roleplay over gunplay: Create storylines first. Legitimate interaction is required before attacking another player.
* RDM: You may not kill or injure another player without prior interaction and a valid reason.
* VDM: You may not intentionally run over other players with a cart, horse, or vehicle.
* Powergaming: Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or mechanics to force outcomes or gain an advantage.
* Metagaming: Do not use outside information, streams, or OOC communication to gain IC advantage.
* Fear RP / NVL: Value your character’s life and act realistically in dangerous situations.
* Revenge killing: If killed, do not return to the scene for revenge. Stay away from the area for at least 1 hour.
* New life rule: If you respawn, you forget the scenario that led to your death.
* Tonics / revive stims: Do not use tonics during active PvP conflict to re-enter a fight.
* Fail RP: Keep speech and actions era-appropriate. Modern language and supernatural roleplay are not allowed.
* Baiting: Do not create fake crimes or act unrealistically just to force law enforcement interaction.
* Vigilantism: Do not interfere with active law enforcement scenes or criminal conflicts you are not part of.
* Hogtie rules: Do not break free from ropes unless allowed by the situation.
* Voice identification: Respect attempts to mask a voice when someone is wearing a mask.
* Medical roleplay: Stay honest and realistic during medical scenes.
* Perma: Permanent death is your decision unless a server-specific rule says otherwise.
* Duels: Accepting a duel means accepting the stated consequences.
* Law and doctor corruption: Corruption must be approved by staff through a ticket.
* Combat logging: Do not log out during active roleplay or medical scenes.
* Communication: A working microphone is required, and all communication must be in English.
* Toxic attitude: Do not bring OOC toxicity into IC or community spaces.
* Items and money: Do not share items or money between your characters.
* Horse theft: Do not use the map or whistle to magically track or reclaim a stolen horse.
* Body dumping: Bodies must be disposed of in a believable way and not dumped into water.
