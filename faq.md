# FAQ

### What PC specs should I have to play on the server? <a href="#what-pc-specs-should-i-have-to-play-on-the-server" id="what-pc-specs-should-i-have-to-play-on-the-server"></a>

To ensure an optimal gaming experience in OaO City Life RP, we recommend the following system specifications as the bare minimum:

* **CPU:** Intel Core i5 3470 @ 3.2GHz or AMD X8 FX-8350 @ 4GHz
* **GPU:** NVIDIA GTX 660 2GB or AMD HD 7870 2GB
* **RAM:** 16GB
* **Storage:** 72GB HDD space + 8GB additional space for updates

These specifications represent the minimum hardware necessary to run the game smoothly. However, for the best possible experience, we strongly recommend having hardware that exceeds these minimum requirements. Higher-end CPUs, GPUs, and additional RAM will provide smoother gameplay, faster loading times, and better overall performance.

For an optimal experience and to fully enjoy all the features and content our server has to offer, we encourage players to consider upgrading their hardware beyond the bare minimum requirements.

***

### Do I need to use a microphone? <a href="#do-i-need-to-use-a-microphone" id="do-i-need-to-use-a-microphone"></a>

Yes, having a microphone is a requirement to play on our server. We encourage players to use a good-quality microphone to enhance communication and immersion during gameplay. Effective communication is essential for engaging in roleplay scenarios, coordinating with other players, and participating in community events.

***

### **Do we have branded vehicles?** <a href="#do-we-have-branded-vehicles" id="do-we-have-branded-vehicles"></a>

No, we do not offer branded vehicles within OaO City Life RP. Following Rockstar Games' purchase of FiveM, there are restrictions prohibiting FiveM servers from utilizing branded vehicles without explicit permission. As a result, we exclusively utilize lore-friendly vehicles to ensure compliance with these regulations.

If you have any further questions regarding our vehicle selection or any other aspects of OaO City Life RP, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance.

***

### Why can't I see any clothing shops, fuel stations, etc., on the map? <a href="#why-cant-i-see-any-clothing-shops-fuel-stations-etc.-on-the-map" id="why-cant-i-see-any-clothing-shops-fuel-stations-etc.-on-the-map"></a>

We have implemented a discovery system so that when you interact with something at a certain place, like an ATM, then that ATM will show up on the map for you. But the important locations like police departments, hospitals, motels, and prisons show for everyone from day 1.

***

#### Why I can't see clothes, buildings, or cars? <a href="#why-i-cant-see-clothes-buildings-or-cars" id="why-i-cant-see-clothes-buildings-or-cars"></a>

Before joining the server for the first time, we would recommend clearing your client cache.

[How to clear cache](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Jq16iEzyOI)

Additionally, if addons are not appearing, it may be because your computer is in the process of downloading all the assets from our server. Once they are downloaded for the first time, they should consistently appear thereafter.
