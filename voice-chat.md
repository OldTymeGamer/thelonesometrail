# Voice chat

The Lonesome Trail uses integrated in-game voice chat. You do not need third-party voice software for roleplay.

### Before you join

1. Select the correct microphone in your system and RedM audio settings.
2. Test your microphone before entering an active scene.
3. Set an audible volume without overpowering nearby players.

### In-game voice range

Press `J` to set your voice range. Use the lowest range that fits the scene.

Speak clearly and stay in character during roleplay. A working microphone is required.

### Voice issues

Check that the correct input device is selected. Restart RedM after changing audio devices.

If voice still fails, open a support ticket with your device and error details. See [troubleshooting-common-issues.md](troubleshooting-common-issues.md "mention") for general checks.
